01 / DESIGN THREADS

三个词，
一种持续的追问。Three words.
One continuing question.

形态如何生成，情绪如何被空间承接，设计又如何回应城市？从课程设计到独立产品，这条线索贯穿朱翰林的作品。

How does form emerge, how can space hold emotion, and how does design answer a city? These questions connect Zhu Hanlin's studio work and independent products.

01

ALGORITHM

泰森多边形、超图蛛网、蚁群路径与波浪纹理——形态不是装饰，而是生成过程的结果。

Voronoi polygons, hypergraph webs, ant-colony paths and wave textures — form as the result of a generative process.

02

EMOTION

从中庭、下沉广场与空中观景台，到一次被认真回答的咨询——为相遇留出位置。

From atriums and sunken plazas to a question answered with care — making room for encounters.

03

CITY

运河遗产、校园门户与低空交通网络——每个项目都从它与城市的关系开始。

Canal heritage, campus gateways and low-altitude networks — every project begins with its relationship to the city.
