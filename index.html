SELECTED WORKS · 2021 — 2026

算法 · 情绪 · 城市 ALGORITHM · EMOTION · CITY

六个项目，围绕三个词展开。「算法」——活动中心的泰森多边形、剧场的超图蛛网、高层的蚁群路径与博物馆的波浪纹理，形态皆由算法生成；「情绪」——城市综合体直面年轻人的情绪困境，以中庭、下沉广场与空中观景台重造相遇；「城市」——从绍兴柯桥的运河遗产、山东科技大学的校园门户，到青岛黄岛的飞行器停靠网络，设计始终与城市对话。

Six projects, three words. ALGORITHM — the student centre's Tyson polygons, the theatre's hypergraph spider-web, the high-rise's ant-colony paths and the museum's wave textures: forms generated by algorithms. EMOTION — the urban complex faces the emotional plight of the young, rebuilding encounters through atrium, sunken plaza and sky terrace. CITY — from the canal heritage of Shaoxing Keqiao and the campus gateway of SDUST to the aircraft docking network of Qingdao Huangdao, the design keeps conversing with the city.
