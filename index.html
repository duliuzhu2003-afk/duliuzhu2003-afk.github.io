SELECTED WORKS · 2021 — 2026

城市 · 建筑 · 记忆 Selected Works of Architecture

建筑于我，是理解城市的方式。五年间，六个项目——从运河边的博物馆，到校园里的活动中心，再到城市中心的高层——构成一段连续的探索：关于记忆，关于场所，也关于建造。

Architecture is my way of reading the city. Six projects over five years — from a museum by the Grand Canal to a student centre on campus, to a high-rise in the urban core — trace a continuous enquiry into memory, place, and construction.

02 — ABOUT ME

关于我 ABOUT

朱翰林，建筑学专业在读。关注城市公共空间与地域文脉，相信好的建筑始于对场所的耐心阅读。课余参与课程设计与竞赛，在图纸与模型之间寻找建筑的秩序。

Zhu Hanlin is a student of architecture with a focus on urban public space and regional context. He believes good architecture begins with a patient reading of place, and works between drawing and model in search of architectural order.
